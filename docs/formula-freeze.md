# Phase 0 definition freeze
registry/formula-inventory.json assigns stable Vn-Fnn IDs to every v0.91 metric, preserving exact definitions. IDs must not be renumbered on insertion; add a new ID and record a versioned decision. FROZEN_V0.91 freezes wording, not executable calculations. All implementations are TBD until their proper phase.

V1 basket membership, weighting, base date, comparable-model taxonomy, proxy approval and interval are TBD. V2 company-specific tags, fiscal alignment and reconciliations are TBD. V3 source-series mappings are TBD; yield inputs are percentage points and 2s10s converts their difference to basis points by multiplying by 100. V4 receipts minus outlays is preserved (negative for deficits); source/units and comparable-auction selection are TBD. V5 table/series, units and vintage mapping are TBD. V6 aggregation windows, regional selection and spot/futures methods are TBD. V7 sector series and wage measure choice are TBD. No economic calculation has been implemented in Phase 0.
